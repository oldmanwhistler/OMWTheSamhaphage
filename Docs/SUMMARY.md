"Every corpse is a new friend."

Adds 5-8 new xenotypes, scenarios and lore based on a "Got to Catch Them All" style gameplay loop around genes and traits. They are intended for player faction as the fun is in harvesting the dead to increase your power. They wouldn't work as a enemy faction because a fresh spawn is weak AF. This is for fans of isekai/litrpg/horror.

This is my first mod for RimWorld. It's all XML with no C# and any non-base game functionality is coming from the required/optional gene mod packs.

The Perfect Stillness is a faction that introduces several xenotypes that have an interwoven reproduction cycle.

The Fluxspawn (brood) are naked vibrating slugs that are impossible to hit. The source of genes and meat when outsiders are scarce.

The Echovessel (thrall) are patchwork salvage-men who devour brains to learn.

The Hallowbound (workers) are tireless, waxy infiltrators that siphon genes, create thralls, maintain the brood, and deal with baseliners and other xenotypes if a non-violent interaction is required.

The Samhaphage (elite) are unmakers that harrow the noise out of blood. Their goal is to evolve and unleash the Absolute Frequency.

The Sovereign Stillness (apex) is the towering obsidian anchor of Perfect Silence and the Absolute Frequency that will harrow the world.

# Game Balance

* Try to collect every trait and gene in the game on one pawn. This will not be balanced, but maybe you will have fun.
* These xenotypes start with nerfs that need to be overcome through gene acquisition.
* The Echovessels are easy to create from the dead with no cool down, but have mood issues without a samhaphage ideology.
* The Hallowbound are close to baseliners, but have methods for gene acquisition and traits.
* The Fluxspawn breed like crazy and are hard to keep alive. They increase colony wealth beyond their utility and let you gamble that risk on the possibility of random genes. They have serious heat issues in early game and could die of hypothermia.
* The Samhaphage run the show with their ability to acquire genes from the living and the dead through biology instead of biotech.
* Raid wealth multipliers means with great power comes great risk.
* The Sovereign Stillnesss xeno is disabled unless you subscribe to the "WVC: Xenotypes and Genes" mod.
* The Sovereign Stillnesss can destroy corpses in melee so while they are OP it also means you have less biomass to harvest after a raid.


# Tip and Tricks

* Pretty hard to maintain mood Without ideology installed unless everyone is a psychopath.
* The Hallowbound have the chill of the dead and enjoy colder temperatures.
* The fluxspawn require heat for their naked, disgusting, fertile bodies.
* The fluxspawn mutate genes daily while the Hallowbound and samhaphage can control when they mutate.
* Try gene integration! Make those xenogenes into endogenes.
* The fluxspawn have a short lifespan and a lot of cancers but make that work for you with tetrogenesis [1] 
* The more fluxspawn you have the more powerful the chimera-like Silent Stillness becomes. The fluxspawn and the Sovereign Stillness form a hive mind with "WVC: Xenotypes and Genes" mod.
* Poor aptitudes abound for fresh colonists. Time to harvest some aptitude genes.
* The samhaphage need raw meat all the time.
* Breeding your hive to kill them to acquire genes is part of the idea! This is why all the xenotypes are psychopaths.
 
[1] Requires optional mod "WVC: Xenotypes and Genes"
