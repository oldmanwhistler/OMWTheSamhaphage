# RimWorld Aesthetic

"A 2D top-down 'pawn' art style characterized by minimalist, limbless characters with simple oval heads and torso-only bodies. The environment features a clean, vector-style grid layout with desaturated, rustic colors—dusty oranges, olive greens, and steel grays. The overall look is a blend of 'Hard Sci-Fi' and 'Space Western,' utilizing thick outlines, simple geometric furniture, and a functional, utilitarian UI aesthetic reminiscent of early 2000s management simulations."

Key Visual Pillars
To get the look exactly right, you have to focus on these four specific constraints:

The "Pawn" Anatomy: Characters lack arms and legs. They are essentially floating torsos with expressive but simple faces. This is often called the "Sub-Version" or "Prison Architect" style.

The Color Palette: It isn't neon or flashy. It uses a "Used Future" palette. Everything looks slightly worn, industrial, or salvaged from a shipwreck.

The Perspective: It is a strictly locked Top-Down (Bird's Eye) perspective, though objects like chairs or workbenches are drawn from a slight ¾ frontal tilt to show detail.

The Environment: Nature is organic and messy (blobs for trees, scattered rocks), while player-built structures are rigid, modular, and perfectly aligned to a square grid.

The center features a glowing, jagged obsidian monolith pulsating with red light. The environment is filled with bio-mechanical containment tubes, flickering fluorescent lights, and 'flesh-beast' creatures made of distorted, lumpy pink textures and many eyes. The color palette is heavy on deep blacks, sickly neon greens, and blood reds, maintaining a clean vector-style grid with thick, dark outlines.

# Sovereign Stillness

The subject is the Sovereign Stillness xenotype. The appearance of the Sovereign Stillness xenotype is taller than the average baseliner and thin. It has two verticle slightly curving Jotun horns coming out of it's mess of tentacled hair-like folicles. Their tentacle hair can be ash grey or snow white. These horns are in a single colour, but that can be charcoal, obsidian, or a rare, regal violet. Their skin is absolute shiny jet chitinous black carapace overlaid with plates of bioferrite armor across its chest and limbs. They have sharp claws, a thin body and a thin tentacle tail. The have no eyes, no eye sockets and no ears. Their mouth takes up a third of their head with razor sharp teeth. There overall head is circular. Their bodies are perfectly androgynous. They carry no weapons but can wear any variety of RimWorld clothing.
