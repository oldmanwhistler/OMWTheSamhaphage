# Xenotype Icon Workflow

## Prompt Workflow

I used free Gemini with my gmail account.

### Create a prompt based on in-game screenshot of the xenotype.

Start a scenario with a ton of your xenotype, get a screenshot, crop it to just the xeno types

> Identify the alien head shapes in this image and write a one paragraph visual description of the shape. Do not reference the filename, the game, different colours, bodies, hair or names. Focus on describing the heads and the variations within. It is very important to describe the shape of the head correctly. Describe any identifying features if they are present like headshape, eye shape, mouth shape, ears, horns, stalks and expressions. Take note of missing eyes, ears or mouth. Be concise.

### Edit that prompt take out elements that are distracting.

Read the thing, rewrite the parts that sound dumb or overemphasize something you don't care about.

### Prompt to create a sheet of 100 icons

> A crisp digital vector sheet on a pure chroma key greenscreen (#00FF00) background, displaying a precise and symmetrical 10x10 grid of monochrome, hand-drawn character face variations. Each small icon adheres to a minimal black and white line art style with a uniform thick, heavy black outline and flat white fills. The edges of every icon are perfectly sharp and aliased, with zero dithering, bleeding, or anti-aliasing artifacts between the black lines and the green background. The composition features 100 unique variations, showcasing a diverse range of head shapes and simple eye configurations (dots, dashes, solid fills, and varying angles) that convey different emotions. Rows 1 through 6 present variations of the same 16 basic face types. Rows 7 through 10 introduce new combinations of eye expressions and head features. The entire grid is perfectly clean, organized, flat, and technical, focusing solely on the subtle differences within the monochrome designs. No duplicate faces, faces only no bodies. Look straight at camera. No noses. No eyebrows.

### Save

Save in `mod\textures\ui\icons\xenotypes` as 64x64 png with transparent background. I use paint.net for that stuff.

## My Xenotypes

### Fluxspawn

A slug's head, shaped is like a fat pear with a BBL. The eye stalks stick straight out left and right. No eyes. Scary mouth. No neck.

### Cradlemold

Bulbous heads that are smoothly rounded or slightly teardrop-shaped (wider at top). The compact, heads serve as the base for a pair of long, dark, and highly flexible antenna- or tentacle-like appendages that curve dramatically outward from the forehead. The head reveals itself as a stubby, snout-like nodule featuring simple, small dark eyes, acting as the central anchor from which the prominent, split sensory stalks emerge (like a slug). No nose.

All the images should include the slug eye stalks poking out the side of the head above eye level. they should also include the two curving head attenae.

The faces are chubby and fat and kind of flirty, some have pink blush anime style.

No eyebrows. Obviously fat.

### Hallowbound

The heads feature a simplistic, stylized, and heavily rounded structure. The primary head shape varies between a smooth, standard oval and a distinct, pronounced upside-down pear shape that tapers sharply down to a narrow, pointed chin. The heads are thin. There are noticeable structural variations across the heads, with some appearing perfectly smooth and circular while others exhibit more blocky, angular jaws or wider, heavier brow lines. Notably, every head completely lacks any visible ears, noses, or mouths, giving them a minimal, blank appearance. The only defined facial features present are the eyes, which are consistently stylized as simple dark eyes, almond-shaped, or slanted slits with neutral, squinting expressions. There is a gauntness to the face. The heads remain looking human, but someone who is sick and wasting away.

### Samhaphage

The heads share a uniform, bulbous head shape resembling an oblong, featureless dome or inverted egg that completely lacks ears, horns, or eye stalks. Eyes are entirely missing across the group. Consequently, their expressions are defined entirely by their mouths: variations on wide gaping black maws packed with sharp menacing teeth. Others show closed smirks with prominent fangs. In most cases the mouth takes up 2/3rd of the head.

### Sovereign Stillness

The entities feature a distinct, standardized head shape defined by a stylized, rounded base adorned with prominent, curved, upward-pointing horns that frame the top of the head. Their head shape is framed by wavy, tentacled hair that flanks the sides of the face and extends downward. Structurally, these figures completely lack visible eyes, ears, or noses. While most faces are entirely blank and featureless, variation exists with some displaying a wide, jagged, toothy grin. 

Remove all eyes, add variations of a wide mouth. the horns are straighter and closer to the center.
